:color[From curiosity to engineering]{value="yellow"}

I was born in Kirov, Russia, in 1992 and grew up in a nearby town. Since childhood, I have been fascinated by :color[engineering mechanisms]{value="white"} and physics, an interest that stayed with me throughout school.

When I was six, my parents bought our first computer. It began as a way to play games, but before long I was modifying game components and the operating system. Building mods and maps led me to HiAsm, a visual, non-coding IDE that became my first step into programming.

As my curiosity grew, I wanted to create new HiAsm components and discovered that I needed languages such as FreePascal or Delphi. I had no internet access at the time, so someone gave me a Delphi 7 book. Creating HiAsm components was still beyond me, but that no longer mattered: :color[I could build my own programs]{value="yellow"} 🎉. ![Photo](/early_photo.jpg "An early photo from my first years learning programming")

That same instinct still guides my work today: understand how a system behaves, take it apart, and rebuild it into something more useful and reliable.
